Availability is the capabilities page, not this skill.
[Capabilities and limits](https://docs.proof.computer/liskov/reference/capabilities)
is the owner. Guides do not widen it.

| Uptime Prober | Release-gated v1. An internal acceptance fixture, not a supported customer offering |
| OpenClaw | Release-gated v1. No versioned descriptor was present. The listing page is missing |
| Third-party Marketplace publishing | Not v1 |

## Uptime Prober bounds

While the gate stands, do not copy the page's option table, defaults, or
prerequisites into a checklist. Link the page. Do not add endpoints. The
page prints no price. Do not invent one. A byte check reads the digest from
the page at the time of the check. Do not store that digest here as a value
to send.

A screenshot plus status, latency, and timestamp is acceptance evidence for
one observation. It is not an uptime service-level agreement and not proof
the origin is trustworthy.

## What review is not

Curated means PROOF controls the v1 catalog. It does not mean the Application
can access external accounts without credentials the user supplies.

Record claims separately. A claim shown as missing, stale, or failed is not
a pass. Not applicable is not a pass. Do not stamp the listing verified or
safe.

Separate Marketplace software price, the per-job cap and reserve, the final
Service Credit charge, and direct external fees such as Telegram. This skill
does not read or invent those numbers. Spend reads are liskov-spend.

## OpenClaw

Do not prepare OpenClaw. The page is missing, and the capabilities row says
no versioned descriptor was present. No options, no endpoints, no launch
procedure, no invented digest.

## After launch

The byte check is incomplete when the listing page prints no digest or the
pin command prints no digest. Do not fill that gap. Do not restore a pin.
Do not publish a replacement. One party's "free" label does not erase
another cost boundary.

`manifestValid` and a local draft are liskov-policy. This skill does not
write a manifest and does not validate one.
